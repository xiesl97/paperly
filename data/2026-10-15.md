Abstract: Equation of state (EOS) tables used in giant planet modeling exhibit regions of strong thermodynamic inconsistency, or disobedience to the first law of thermodynamics. These generally result from smooth interpolation over physical discontinuities or between thermodynamic sub-domains governed by different physical models. The effect of thermodynamic inconsistency on structure models of planets evolved with these EOS has never been studied in detail, although it is understood to introduce spurious sources or sinks of energy or entropy in the models, leading to inaccuracies.
